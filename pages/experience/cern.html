CERN · Machine learning & FPGA research

Selected for CERN openlab, I studied lightweight jet reconstruction from ATLAS calorimeter data, considering the bandwidth and FPGA constraints of a future Trigger-Level Analysis system.

What I worked on

  • Developed a convolutional baseline with 708 trainable parameters, then compared candidate-level attention and smaller model variants.
  • Evaluated reconstruction with and without pile-up and estimated the bandwidth of a compact jet list. Hardware synthesis, FPGA resource use and latency measurements remained future work.

Results

The baseline reached 77.3% efficiency at 78.0% purity. Candidate-level attention increased efficiency to approximately 80.5%. The 80-value output per event was estimated at 2.1 Gb/s per board, below the assumed 20 Gb/s budget.
