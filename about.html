About me

I grew up in Brazil and found in aerospace a reason to keep asking questions. Public education, persistence and scholarships brought me to UFSM, then to Grenoble for a dual degree in embedded systems.

That journey shapes what I build. Alongside technical work, I helped create EP Aero and coordinated science outreach. I want more students to see a place for themselves in engineering, especially when that path feels far away.
