BRAFITEC · Grenoble · 2023 to 2025

Finding my way to Grenoble

Selection for BRAFITEC, studying in French and a dual degree in embedded systems.

StoryGrenoble, France2023 to 2025
In this story

In 2023, I was selected for BRAFITEC to study embedded systems at Grenoble INP. Getting there involved two applications, preparing for the French exam and organising the move alongside my studies at UFSM. Those two years expanded my technical training and gave me experience studying and working in an international environment.

01

Preparing the application

BRAFITEC offered a full scholarship for two years of engineering studies in France. For the selection I applied to, academic performance, research experience, publications and language requirements mattered. I prepared my application alongside research in electronics, computing and satellites.

In 2022, I was placed on the waiting list and did not get a place. I continued my studies and research, then applied again in the next selection.

02

Selection for Grenoble

In 2023, I was accepted and could choose Grenoble and embedded systems, the combination I had been aiming for. The time between applications allowed me to build more experience in programming and research.

03

The French language requirement

My acceptance was conditional on submitting an official French certificate by the end of the month. I devoted that period to intensive preparation, took the exam in Curitiba and achieved the required score.

Passing the exam met the scholarship requirement. Following technical classes and communicating in French every day was a separate learning process that continued after I arrived.

04

Preparing the move

I arranged my passport, visa and travel while completing the semester at UFSM and continuing my research at ITA. It meant coordinating deadlines and academic commitments, with support from professors and classmates.

05

The first month in France

The first month included orientation activities and daily French classes. Meeting other exchange students helped me settle into the routine and practise the language beyond the classroom.

I also had time to visit Paris and get to know the country a little. Those outings were a relaxed way to practise French and build friendships.

06

Adapting to technical classes

Studying in French required a new routine. I took notes on unfamiliar terms, reviewed the material at home and gradually became more comfortable following the classes.

The move from aerospace engineering to embedded systems also meant strengthening my foundations in electronics and computing. Conversations with classmates helped me understand the grading system and adjust my preparation for exams.

07

Life in Grenoble

As I settled into the course, I organised my study time better and made room for sport. Grenoble made it easy to enjoy hiking and skiing with classmates. That balance became part of my routine.

08

An international exchange

Studying with people from different countries brought me into contact with other approaches to learning and everyday life. Group work, conversations and trips were opportunities to practise languages and learn from those differences.

Par avion · What I learned

I came back with a degree in embedded systems, internship experience and friends I still keep in touch with. I also learned to balance studying with life outside university. Grenoble remains a place I remember fondly.
