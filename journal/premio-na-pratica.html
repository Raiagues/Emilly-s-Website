Na Prática · São Paulo · November 2025

My experience at the Na Prática Award

Winner for the Southern Region in 2025, with training, mentoring and exchanges with other finalists.

StorySão Paulo, BrazilNovember 2025
In this story
Presenting my workThe final in São PauloTraining and mentoring at BTG PactualThe Southern Region awardConnections after the awardPress coverage

I applied just a few days before the deadline. Writing the application made me pause and look at my projects together, rather than as separate tasks I had already moved on from. Months later, I was in São Paulo for the final. The award was part of that experience, and so were the conversations and friendships that stayed with me.

Awards and press

01

Presenting my work

I heard about the Prêmio Na Prática Protagonismo Universitário through Fundação Estudar. They shared the opportunity with our community. At first, I wasn’t going to apply. Then, very close to the deadline, I decided to try.

The process itself was already worth it. Writing about my experiences made me notice things I hadn’t stopped to see while I was living them. It wasn’t that I had forgotten what I’d done. I just hadn’t looked at those moments together, with a little distance.

02

The final in São Paulo

Na Prática covered travel and accommodation for the in-person final in São Paulo. Bringing the finalists together made it easier to exchange experiences throughout the programme.

03

Training and mentoring at BTG Pactual

The final took place at BTG Pactual. Alongside the assessment panel, we had training and mentoring sessions. Hearing about projects from other fields was useful for thinking about how I present and develop my own work.

04

The Southern Region award

The ceremony was on 3 November 2025, and I won the award for the South region. There are photos of me waiting for the result, speaking on stage and holding the trophy. Seeing them still brings that moment back.

Winning was wonderful, of course. But I would also have been happy to see another finalist from the South receive it. They were incredible people with incredible stories. I never experienced those days as a competition between us. I was already so happy just to be there.

The result published by Na Prática

05

Connections after the award

I stayed in touch with other finalists and people from Fundação Estudar. Some of those friendships continued on the trips to China and Brazil at Silicon Valley, with new opportunities to exchange ideas.

06

Press coverage

Then something happened that I hadn’t imagined either. We appeared on the cover of EXAME. My university, UFSM, also published an article about me and my story.

People started stopping me at the supermarket to say they had seen the news and to congratulate me. It was funny. I felt like a celebrity. Such an unexpected, lovely part of it all.

The EXAME feature

My story on the UFSM website

Par avion · What I learned

Writing the application had already been valuable. I had to recognise work I had not stopped to appreciate while doing it. Winning the South Region award made me proud, and the group gave those days another meaning. Some of us met again in China and the United States. I like seeing how an application that began with my own projects became a story shared with other people.
