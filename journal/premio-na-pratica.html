Na Prática · São Paulo · November 2025

My experience at the Na Prática Award

Winner for the Southern Region in 2025, with training, mentoring and exchanges with other finalists.

From my personal photo album

In November 2025, I received the Southern Region award at the Prêmio Na Prática Protagonismo Universitário. The selection was an opportunity to present my university work, take part in mentoring and exchange experiences with finalists from different fields.

Awards and press

01

Presenting my work

I learned about the award through Fundação Estudar. Preparing the application required bringing together my projects, responsibilities and results in a clear account of my university experience.

That preparation helped me connect activities I had developed in different contexts and communicate what I had contributed to each one.

02

The final in São Paulo

Na Prática covered travel and accommodation for the in-person final in São Paulo. Bringing the finalists together made it easier to exchange experiences throughout the programme.

03

Training and mentoring at BTG Pactual

The final took place at BTG Pactual. Alongside the assessment panel, we had training and mentoring sessions. Hearing about projects from other fields was useful for thinking about how I present and develop my own work.

04

The Southern Region award

At the ceremony on 3 November 2025, I received the Southern Region award. I am proud of that recognition and of the university work I was able to present during the selection.

The award also included the immersion trip to China in January 2026. That experience has its own page in this journal.

The result published by Na Prática

05

Connections after the award

I stayed in touch with other finalists and people from Fundação Estudar. Some of those friendships continued on the trips to China and Brazil at Silicon Valley, with new opportunities to exchange ideas.

06

Press coverage

The award recipients appeared on the cover of EXAME, and UFSM also published an article about my work. The coverage brought my projects to audiences beyond the university.

The EXAME feature

Par avion · What I learned

I remember those days for the award, the mentoring and the group that stayed in touch. Some of those friendships continued on the trips to China and the United States. It is nice to find familiar faces on new journeys.
