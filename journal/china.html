China · Beijing & Shanghai · 25 to 31 January 2026

A week of technology visits in China

Technology visits, new food and time with the group in Beijing and Shanghai.

The trip to China was part of the Na Prática Award. In January 2026, I spent a week in Beijing and Shanghai with the group, visiting companies and getting to know a little of the cities between activities.

The award behind this trip

01

The journey to Beijing

The immersion took place from 25 to 31 January 2026, in Beijing and Shanghai, as part of the Na Prática Award. We travelled for about 25 hours, including a connection in Doha, before beginning the visits.

Na Prática’s account of our immersion

02

Meeting the group and organisers

FGCI, which organised the immersion, welcomed us with a dinner and an introduction to the programme. An interpreter accompanied the visits and helped us follow the presentations and discussions.

03

A varied schedule

We had three or four visits a day, covering different technologies and organisations. Taking photos and discussing what we saw with the group helped me organise the information. The meals were also a chance to try local food.

04

Hardware, robotics and space

The visits included SpaceSail, Alibaba, Huawei and WhalesBot, alongside organisations in education, healthcare and entrepreneurship. I was particularly interested in the hardware, robotics and space applications.

We saw educational robotics kits, a robot drawing a portrait and a small car presented as controlled by signals from a device worn on the head. The demonstrations offered concrete examples of how the technologies were being presented and applied.

05

Processes in healthcare

At a hospital, I was interested in how the different processes worked together. The visit included digital triage and a medication transport system. It was a chance to look at technology through the organisation of a service.

06

Adapting the questions

In some visits, more specific questions led to clearer answers than broad ones. A conversation at Suzano about negotiation in China also helped me pay attention to the context of each exchange. This was a lesson from those particular meetings, rather than a general rule about the country.

07

A visit to the Great Wall

The schedule also included time to visit the Great Wall and explore the cities with the group. Those outings added cultural context to the technical programme.

08

The final days in Shanghai

We finished the trip in Shanghai, with a group dinner overlooking the illuminated city. I stayed in touch with people from the immersion and continued some of the conversations we had begun during the visits.

Par avion · What I learned

It was a busy week, with visits across different fields and plenty to talk about with the group. I remember the robots, the Great Wall and our last dinner in Shanghai. I still keep in touch with people I met there.
