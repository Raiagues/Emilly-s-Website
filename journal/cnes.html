Six months in Toulouse with CNES

A student satellite, the team and life in Toulouse during my last semester in France.

In 2025, I spent six months in Toulouse working on flight software for AEROSAT at CNES. It was my last semester in France, with a new team, friends from different countries and a city I enjoyed getting to know.

My flight software work at CNES

01

Looking for a technical internship

In my final year in France, I was looking for a full-time, six-month internship before returning to Brazil to finish aerospace engineering. I wanted an opportunity to work with satellites or launch vehicles.

I sent around 200 applications, attended career fairs and took part in interviews. The search helped me understand the different roles in the space sector and the requirements of each opportunity.

02

Choosing the satellite project

I received offers from CERN and CNES. The CERN project involved physics and electronics, with a team I had enjoyed meeting during the selection process.

At CNES, I would work on a student satellite alongside other interns. The project was close to what I wanted to learn, and I liked the idea of being part of that team.

I chose CNES for the chance to work directly on the satellite. That was in 2025. The CERN openlab summer programme, which has its own story in this journal, came in 2026.

03

Flight software for AEROSAT

AEROSAT is a student nanosatellite project at CNES within Nanolab Academy. Working on a satellite intended for launch gave me practical experience with the requirements of this kind of system.

My work focused on flight software, including recovering information after a restart and organising tasks without delaying initialisation. The technical page explains the memory and implementation details.

About AEROSAT and Nanolab Academy, on the CNES website

04

Autonomy and technical guidance

I had autonomy in my tasks and regular guidance from my supervisors. Weekly meetings were a chance to discuss progress, ask questions and understand more about the agency’s work.

05

Aerospace in Toulouse

Living in Toulouse was also an opportunity to visit aerospace museums and exhibitions. I saw spacecraft and aircraft displays, including an A380 and Concorde. I enjoyed connecting those visits with subjects I had studied.

06

The interns and life in Toulouse

The group of interns included people from France and other countries. Alongside work, we explored the city and went on outings together. Those exchanges helped me practise French and get to know people from different backgrounds.

07

Experience for the next step

The internship gave me practical experience in flight software and a clearer view of satellite development. After returning to Brazil, I continued in the space sector at Visiona, where I also developed an interest in integration and testing.

Par avion · What I learned

At CNES, I gained experience in flight software and learned from a team that gave us room to ask questions and try things out. I remember the work on AEROSAT as fondly as the time spent with friends in Toulouse.
