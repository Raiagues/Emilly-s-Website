Fundação Estudar · travel diary · 6 to 12 April 2026

My first time in the United States

Technology, research and entrepreneurship at Brazil at Silicon Valley and Brasil Project.

In April 2026, I visited the United States for the first time. I took part in Brazil at Silicon Valley with support from Fundação Estudar, then continued to Boston for Brasil Project. Between events, I visited universities and spent time with friends.

01

Selection and travel preparation

On 27 February 2026, I was selected for Brazil at Silicon Valley with financial support from Fundação Estudar. With just over a month before the trip, I arranged the visa through an emergency appointment and completed the travel preparations.

02

Brazil at Silicon Valley

The event connects people from Brazil with Silicon Valley’s technology and entrepreneurship community. The 2026 edition took place at Google MP7 in Sunnyvale from 6 to 8 April.

I spoke with entrepreneurs, investors and researchers, including people working in AI and law. Hearing how they approached their projects gave me useful perspectives beyond aerospace engineering.

About Brazil at Silicon Valley

03

Visits to universities and companies

Between events, I visited Stanford, Berkeley and Meta. Seeing these places in person added context to institutions I already knew through their research and technology.

04

Brasil Project in Cambridge

I continued to Cambridge for the first Brasil Project, held from 10 to 12 April at Harvard and MIT. That part of the trip was self-funded, separate from Fundação Estudar’s support for Brazil at Silicon Valley.

The programme brought together discussions about Brazil, technology, business and public life. I also visited both universities during the event.

Brasil Project · 2026 programme

05

A conversation at MIT AeroAstro

At MIT, I spoke with Professor Kerri Cahoy from AeroAstro about research areas including satellites. She leads the STAR Lab, which works on technologies such as small satellites and laser communications. The conversation connected the visit directly with subjects I study and work on.

Kerri Cahoy · MIT AeroAstro

06

Time with the group

There was also time to explore California with the group. We cycled near the Golden Gate and attended a basketball game featuring Brazilian player Gui Santos. I kept these photos alongside the event records as part of the trip.

07

Ideas to try in my projects

During the trip, I tried Claude for the first time. The experience and conversations about AI encouraged me to experiment with new tools in my own projects.

I returned wanting to test ideas in smaller iterations and seek feedback earlier. Hearing how people from other fields work helped me consider different ways to develop and present technical projects.

Par avion · What I learned

I came back with ideas to try in my projects and contacts from different fields. I enjoyed getting to know the campuses and hearing how other people work. I would like to go back and continue some of those conversations.
