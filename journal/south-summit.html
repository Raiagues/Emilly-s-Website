It started with a conversation with Verônica, my mentor. I told her I wanted to get closer to people in the space industry, hear about their work and understand the paths they had taken. I wasn’t imagining that this conversation would lead to organising and moderating a panel.

01

An idea from my mentor

I met Verônica Allende Serra through Lyfe Academy, and she has helped me in many parts of my journey. When I told her what I was looking for, she said she was on the South Summit board and would talk to the organisers about the possibility of me putting together a panel on the space sector.

The 2026 event took place from March 25 to 27 at Cais Mauá, in Porto Alegre. For me, the work started well before those three days.

South Summit Brazil · 2026 edition

02

Research, messages and invitations

I started researching the sector, mapping out people I wanted to invite and thinking about the conversation I wanted to bring to the stage. Then came the messages and the introductions. Some people couldn’t take part, but there were two guests I especially hoped would say yes.

One was Danilo Miranda. His work included systems engineering and project management on VCUB1 at Visiona, followed by leadership of the RATO-14X hypersonic launch vehicle project at Mac Jee.

The other was Thais Cardoso Franco, co-founder and CEO of Quasar Space, with a master’s and doctorate from ITA. I admired her technical knowledge and the story of building her startup with her own resources. I really wanted to hear from her.

I wanted to get closer to the space sector, and organising the panel gave me a reason to reach out with something concrete. I needed to understand each person’s work and think about what they could bring to the conversation. The preparation itself was already a way of learning about the field.

Danilo Miranda · professional background

Thais Cardoso Franco · Quasar Space

03

And then we were on stage

They both accepted. There were some last-minute complications and, for a while, Danilo’s participation was uncertain. In the end, it worked out. Getting to moderate that conversation after all the preparation was very special.

My photos also capture the panel “Aerospace Innovation Shaping New Markets”, which I moderated with Jorgito Stocchero, from AEL Sistemas. Looking at those images brings back the feeling of being there, asking questions of people whose experience I wanted to learn from.

04

People I got to meet

One of the conversations I loved was with Mariana Chaves. She had studied at the military school in Santa Maria, the same city as my university, and had been accepted to Harvard with a full scholarship. That connection to Santa Maria made meeting her even more special to me.

I also met Eduardo Leite, governor of Rio Grande do Sul at the time of the event, and Marcelo Lacerda, one of the co-founders of Terra. There were so many different paths and experiences in one place.

And there was Jade Picon. We are the same age, and I had followed her since I was about twelve or thirteen. It felt as if I had watched her grow up while I was growing up too, even though we had never met. Hearing about her work as an entrepreneur in person was such a different experience.

Marcelo Lacerda · entrepreneurial background

05

The conversations kept going

Our host was Marina Sirotsky, a board member at Maromar Investimentos with experience in family governance at Grupo RBS. Staying at her home and spending time together outside the event are part of the memories I keep from those days.

I met people from other countries, and an investor introduced me to other founders in the space sector. I had wanted to get closer to people in the industry, and that was happening through conversations, introductions and the work of bringing the panel together.

Marina Sirotsky · Maromar

It all began with telling Verônica something I wanted to understand better. Her support opened a door, and then came the research, the invitations and the work of putting the panel together. That is what I take from this experience: being specific about what I want to learn helps other people help me. And when an opportunity appears, I can start with the next concrete task, even before I feel completely ready.
