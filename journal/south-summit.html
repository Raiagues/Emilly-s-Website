At South Summit Brazil 2026, I organised and moderated conversations about the space sector. The work began with a suggestion from my mentor and involved research, invitations and preparing questions for the guests.

01

A suggestion from my mentor

I met Verônica Allende Serra through Lyfe Academy. When we discussed my interest in learning more about the space sector, she suggested proposing a panel to South Summit and helped connect me with the organisers.

The event took place from 25 to 27 March 2026 at Cais Mauá in Porto Alegre. Preparation began before the event, with research into the topics and possible speakers.

South Summit Brazil · 2026 edition

02

Research and invitations

I researched the sector, identified speakers and planned the topics for discussion. Coordinating invitations and availability was part of turning the proposal into a panel.

The guests included Danilo Miranda, with experience in systems engineering and management of VCUB1 at Visiona and leadership of the RATO-14X project at Mac Jee, and Thais Cardoso Franco, cofounder of Quasar Space with a master’s degree and doctorate from ITA.

Danilo Miranda · professional background

Thais Cardoso Franco · Quasar Space

03

Moderating the panels

I moderated the conversation with Thais Cardoso Franco and Danilo Miranda. Preparing the questions helped me connect their different experiences within the discussion.

I also moderated “Aerospace Innovation Shaping New Markets” with Jorgito Stocchero from AEL Sistemas. These panels gave me practical experience in preparing and leading technical conversations for an event audience.

04

Conversations across fields

Beyond the panels, I met people from research, business and public life. The photos record some of those encounters, including Mariana Chaves, Marcelo Lacerda, Eduardo Leite and Jade Picon. I enjoyed hearing perspectives beyond my own field.

Marcelo Lacerda · entrepreneurial background

05

Contacts that continued

The event also brought introductions to other space-sector entrepreneurs and conversations with people from different countries. Organising the panel was a practical way to get closer to the sector and build contacts through shared work.

Marina Sirotsky · Maromar

Par avion · What I learned

Preparing the panel involved research, invitations and help from my mentor. I enjoyed moderating the conversations and learning about the guests’ work. Some of those contacts continued after the event.
